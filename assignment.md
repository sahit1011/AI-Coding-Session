## Exercise Overview

Thank you for taking the time to work on the Dexicon AI take home assessment. We've designed this exercise to be highly relevant to the type of work you'll be doing if you join the team.

- **Time limit:** 3 hours maximum. Going a few minutes over is fine, but please stay close to this limit. The constraint helps us respect your time while also letting you demonstrate focus and restraint in scoping an MVP.
- **What we're evaluating:** Your ability to take an open-ended problem, scope an MVP, and deliver a working end-to-end solution. Polish isn't important. 
- **AI tools:** Use AI assistants as you normally would. We'll discuss your code afterward, so be prepared to explain your decisions and reason deeply about your implementation.

## Your Task

Build a prototype for a feature that lets engineers search their company's AI coding assistant history. Your goal is to create a minimum viable search experience.

**Sample data provided:** We've provided AI coding sessions from Claude Code for three imaginary engineers (Dan, Andrew, Diana) who work at a live video streaming company. The data is split across three files only due to email attachment size limits. It accurately represents Claude Code's local storage format. You may add more data using the same schemas.

### Core Requirements:

- Users can search the entire dataset of AI coding sessions (including their teammates)
- Search results are returned and displayed to the user

### Everything else is your judgment:

- **Scope of features:** Decide what's minimum viable and make sure you complete that first. Then extend if you have time.
- **Technology choices:** Use what you think is the best fit and what you are most comfortable with. You can use any dependencies or libraries you want to.
- **Search approach:** Your choice given the 3-hour time limit you have (keyword search, semantic search, natural language search, hybrid, or something else)
- **User Interface:** It's your decision on what the user interface and experience is: API, CLI, lightweight web UI, etc. Choose what you're confident you can complete and what best showcases your strengths. You decide how to display and order results.
